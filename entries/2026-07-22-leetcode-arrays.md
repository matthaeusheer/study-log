## What I studied

Cycled the core patterns until fast and error-free:
- Opposing pointers on a sorted array (two-sum, container with most water)
- Sliding window with a shrinking left edge (longest substring without repeats)
- Fast/slow pointers (cycle detection, middle of list)

## Key insight

Most two-pointer problems reduce to: what invariant lets me move a pointer inward without missing the answer? Name the invariant first, then the code is obvious.
