## What I Did
* Read up on establishing a framework for system design interviews
* Read up back-of-envelope-estimation

## Key Insights
* 4-step System Design Interview Framework
1) Step 1: Understand the problem and establish design scope (interview the interviewer) 3-10 min
2) Step 2: Propose high-level design and get buy-in (collaborate, communicate) 10-15 min
3) Step 3: Design Deep Dive (for selected components/areas) 10-25 min
4) Step 4: Wrap-Up (improvements, error cases, extensions, summary, operations) 3-5 min
* Common estimation properties: #Users, QPS, Peak QPS, Storage size, cache size, Availability, Latency, ...
