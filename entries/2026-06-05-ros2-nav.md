## What I studied

- Global vs. local costmaps and how the planner + controller consume them
- Behavior trees driving the navigation state machine
- Lifecycle nodes: explicit configure/activate/deactivate transitions

## Key insight

Moving from ROS 1's central master to ROS 2's DDS discovery removes a single point of failure but makes network config (multicast, domains) something you actually have to reason about.
